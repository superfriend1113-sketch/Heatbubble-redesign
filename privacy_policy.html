Privacy Policy for HeatBubble

Last updated: March 31, 2026

1. Introduction

HeatBubble ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your email address and basic profile information.
  • Temperature Data: Temperature readings collected by your device sensors.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the app, including features accessed and settings preferences.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers.
  • Analytics Data: App performance, crash reports, and usage statistics via Firebase Analytics.
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction data collected by Google AdMob for personalized advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the HeatBubble service
  • Sync your temperature data across devices
  • Send notifications about temperature changes
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions
  • Display relevant advertisements (for non-premium users)
  • Analyze app usage and fix technical issues

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Local Storage: Temperature readings are stored locally on your device using encrypted database (Isar).
  • Cloud Storage: When signed in, aggregated data is synced to Google Cloud Firestore with industry-standard encryption.
  • Security Measures: We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

5. Third-Party Services

HeatBubble uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Services: For analytics, cloud storage, and authentication (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Billing: For in-app purchases (Privacy Policy)

6. Advertising

We display advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to provide personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings or visiting Google Ad Settings.

7. Data Retention

  • Temperature Data: Stored locally until you delete the app or clear app data. Cloud-synced data is retained while your account is active.
  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account or request data deletion.
  • Analytics Data: Retained according to Firebase Analytics retention policies (typically 14 months).

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Export your temperature data

9. Children's Privacy

HeatBubble is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
