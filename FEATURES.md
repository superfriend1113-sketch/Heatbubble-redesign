# HeatBubble Features: Free vs Premium

## 🆓 FREE TIER

### Core Temperature Tracking
- ✅ Real-time temperature monitoring using device sensors
- ✅ Temperature readings every 15 minutes (background polling)
- ✅ 7-day temperature history
- ✅ Temperature trend detection (Rising/Falling/Stable)
- ✅ Variance calculation (last 20 readings)
- ✅ All three temperature units (°C, °F, K)
- ✅ Switch between units anytime

### Charts & Visualization
- ✅ 12-hour hourly temperature chart
- ✅ Visual temperature trends
- ✅ Temperature state indicators (Normal/Elevated/Low)

### Notifications
- ✅ Temperature alerts (extreme hot/cold)
- ✅ Trend alerts (significant changes)
- ✅ Comparison notifications (vs. hours ago, yesterday, weekly average)
- ✅ Daily reminders (optional)
- ✅ All notifications respect selected temperature unit

### Basic Features
- ✅ Home screen widget (shows temperature on home screen)
- ✅ Activity history view
- ✅ Notification history
- ✅ Settings customization
- ✅ Sensor preferences
- ✅ Test notifications

### Account & Sync
- ✅ Firebase authentication (Google Sign-In, Email/Password)
- ✅ Manual cloud sync
- ✅ Cloud statistics view

### Limitations (Free Tier)
- ⚠️ Ads displayed:
  - App open ad (once per session on launch)
  - Banner ad on home screen (bottom)
  - Interstitial ads when:
    - Tapping temperature unit buttons (°C, °F, K) on home screen
    - Toggling notification settings
    - Changing sensor preferences
    - Switching temperature units in settings
- ⚠️ No custom temperature alerts
- ⚠️ Gentle upgrade prompts after certain number of readings
- ⚠️ Home widget shows basic data only

## 🔄 SUBSCRIPTION MANAGEMENT

### How to Subscribe
1. Open HeatBubble app
2. Go to Settings
3. Tap "Upgrade" button in Subscription section
4. Choose Monthly or Annual plan
5. Complete purchase via Google Play

### How to Restore Purchases
1. Go to Settings
2. Scroll to Subscription section
3. Tap "Restore Purchases" button
4. Your premium status will be restored

### How to Cancel
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Go to Subscriptions
3. Find HeatBubble
4. Tap "Cancel subscription"
5. Follow prompts

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: Can I try Premium before paying?
**A:** Yes! Both plans include a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime during the trial period.

### Q: What happens if I cancel my subscription?
**A:** You'll keep Premium features until the end of your billing period, then revert to Free tier.

### Q: Can I switch between Monthly and Annual plans?
**A:** Yes! Cancel your current plan and subscribe to the other. You'll be charged the new rate at the next billing cycle.

### Q: Will my data be deleted if I cancel Premium?
**A:** No! Your local data stays on your device. Cloud data remains accessible if you resubscribe.

### Q: Do ads come back immediately after canceling?
**A:** Ads return when your subscription period ends (not immediately upon cancellation).

### Q: Can I get a refund?
**A:** Refunds are handled by Google Play Store. Contact Google Play support for refund requests.
